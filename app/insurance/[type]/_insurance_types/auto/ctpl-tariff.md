### Coverages – Compulsory Third Party Liability (CTPL)

| # | Coverage Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | Limit of liability for CTPL (Sections I & II) for all vehicle types is mandated by law at **Php 200,000.00** |
| 2 | CTPL cover may be issued for all motor vehicle types, notwithstanding Prohibited Risks, as it is mandated by law |
| 3 | **Certificate of Cover (COC)** is the document submitted to the LTO as evidence of CTPL cover |
| 4 | COCs must be authenticated through the **ISAP COC Authentication System (COCAS)** and verified by COCAF before the LTO will accept them |
| 5 | Once issued and released, a CTPL cover **cannot be cancelled**. Authentication and/or documentary stamp costs (if cancelled beyond the issuance month) are for the account of the intermediary |
